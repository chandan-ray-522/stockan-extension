Stockan for Zerodha Kite

Option chain analytics, Order Flow and market-structure tools on top of Kite web, built for learning how the market behaves.

Option chain analyticsMarket stance, PCR, max pain, OI change, OI build-up and key support and resistance, all in one panel.
Order Flow and chart toolsFootprint, Smart Money Concepts, RSI with divergence, volume profile, anchored VWAP and market profile.
Index FlowPrice, breadth and order-book pressure of the stocks inside an index, in one speedometer.
AI AnalysisOne combined reading of option chain, order book, price action, RSI and news, with a reversal watch and expected range. Optional AI explanation with your own Gemini key.
News, alerts and paper tradingNews sentiment, MTM Guard alerts and virtual paper orders to practise without risk.
You stay in controlStockan never places an order. Buy and Sell only open Kite's own order window and you confirm there.

Your data stays with you

Your Kite Connect keys and login session are stored in your own browser. Stockan has no server that receives your data. Read the Privacy Policy.

You need your own Zerodha account and a Kite Connect app. The Setup Guide takes about five minutes.
